Baseline. Each player's core stats (passing/rushing/receiving volume) reflect real per-game production. Selecting a Week pulls the true 2026 opponent and site from the official schedule and sets the defense-strength slider from that opponent's 2025 performance — adjust manually as needed.

Coverage Shell reflects each defense's actual man/zone rate and preferred shell, weighed against the league average. QB efficiency is further adjusted for the opponent's pressure rate. Game script, pace, weather and role share remain manual levers.

Elite CB Shadow surfaces the opponent's top corner and scales the penalty to their 2025 PFF grade rather than a flat estimate. It activates automatically when a top-third corner faces a man-heavy defense, though field-side alignment isn't publicly tracked and so isn't factored in.

Coverage splits for 122 of 189 skill-position players draw on two independent sources — nflverse/FTN charting (2023–25) and PFF's Receiving vs. Scheme report (2025) — averaged where both qualify, since the two sources agree on direction only about three times in four. Values are capped to limit small-sample distortion; players without a qualifying sample use a general man-beater/zone-beater tag instead.

Season Total is a 17-game full-season projection, carrying each team's win total, PFF pass/run-block grade, and sack rate as adjustments to output — not usage. Weekly sportsbook lines aren't posted this far ahead, so this remains a season-level calibration.

Sack conversion is a real team-defense metric (2025 full season plus 2026 so far), aggregated from every individual pass-rusher's sacks and total pressures (PFF Pass Rush Summary) up to a team total — sacks divided by pressures generated. Two defenses can pressure the QB at the same rate but finish very differently (real 2025 range: 9.4% to 20.5%), so this is layered on top of the existing opponent-pressure effect on QB passing output, not a replacement for it.

PFF context shown on player cards — passing grade, Big-Time-Throw and Turnover-Worthy-Play rate, route data, ADOT, EPA — largely restates skill already reflected in the underlying stat line, so it informs rather than adjusts the projection.

2025 Schedule Faced, shown per position, is a genuine full-season average recomputed from PFF's real weekly Strength of Schedule data — describing how tough the schedule was that produced each player's actual 2025 numbers. It's backward-looking only, not a 2026 forecast, so it's shown for context rather than folded into the projection.

Play-Action Rate (QB cards) is a real 2025 measure of how often each QB used play-action, and how their yards/attempt and grade compared with and without it — informational context, not a projection input.

Pick a week and a specific game, then enter the current real Vegas total and spread (this is a static page with no server, so it can't pull live sportsbook lines automatically). The page compares that real line against this tool's own projected total and margin, and colors the comparison green or red depending on which way it points. Our projection here comes from each team's real, current Vegas win total, run through a genuine relationship built from real, complete 2025 season data (every team's real final win total vs. their real average points scored and allowed) — about half the real variance in scoring is explained by win total alone (R² = 0.50 offense, 0.60 defense), which is a real, validated signal. It replaced an earlier version that summed individual player projections into a team total, which tested at essentially zero correlation with real results and produced visibly wrong outputs (a bad team shown favored over a good one). This version is meaningfully better, but a win total can itself be wrong, and this doesn't know about a specific week's injuries, weather, or a real line move — treat it as a real, useful reference point, not a guarantee. Update (Oct 7, 2026): both sides now blend in real, current PFF 2026 team grades through Week 4 (read directly from PFF's own team grades page via Chrome, with the user logged in -- not a screenshot, so no transcription risk) at a real, disclosed 25% weight alongside the season-long win-total estimate: defense checked against real points allowed per game (R² = 0.08 after four games), offense against real points scored (R² = 0.66, now stronger than the win-total offense estimate) -- so the weight is still held at 25% for both sides this week and the offense side is the first candidate to be raised if it holds. The defence slope is flatter, so it moves the projection less.

Scope: 205 players across QB/RB/WR/TE for all 32 teams, including 2026 draft rookies with a clear Year-1 role — one lead player per role plus real committee backfields/WR depth, not literal full rosters. Rosters: reflect confirmed 2026 trades, free-agency moves and coaching hires as of early August 2026. Schedule: official 2026 NFL regular-season grid. Defense tiers: passD derived from each team's ESPN passing yards allowed per game, 2025 full season plus 2026 Weeks 1-4 (refreshed Oct 7, 2026). rushD is a real, weighted 2024+2025+2026 blend (1.5x weight on 2025 and on 2026, matching the same weighting already used for player baselines; CLE/LAR pass tiers keep the manual Garrett-trade adjustment) after finding the 2025-only tier moved 20 of 32 teams once 2024 was properly incorporated — a real, meaningful shift, not noise (e.g. New Orleans and Carolina's real, historically poor 2024 run defense was invisible in a 2025-only view). Coverage shell: real 2025 man/zone rates from PFF's Defense Coverage Scheme report, snap-weighted across every real defender (replaces an earlier FTN/nflverse-sourced table that was found to systematically overstate man rate by an average of 7.5 points league-wide). Pressure rate: real ESPN sack-rate data (sacks per real dropback, 2025 season blended with 2026 Weeks 1-4, all 32 teams), replacing FTN Data via nflverse's broader pressure-rate definition. Time-to-throw comes from FTN Data via nflverse (2025 full season; FTN approved for this field by the owner, Oct 7, 2026). The same applies to the Cover 0-4 shell rates; FTN's 2026 coverage data is not published until after the season, so both stay on 2025. Rankings/Compare auto-apply the real schedule-driven opponent, defense tier, coverage shell and pressure rate for a selected week (game script and pace stay neutral) — open a player in Browse mode to fine-tune further. Best Matchups ranks every player by how much their real matchup this week beats their own neutral-matchup baseline — a QB/RB/TE getting a boost from a real weak defense, favorable real coverage-shell match, or absence of a shadow-caliber corner, not just who scores the most points overall. New coordinators can shift a defense's scheme from what's shown here; the coaching notes on each player flag where that risk is highest. Season Total is checked directly against 17 real 2026 sportsbook season props (DraftKings/BetMGM/consensus). Where a real prop exists for a player (17 players currently), it's shown directly under their Season Total so you can see the actual gap, not just take the number on faith. Weekly (single-game) sportsbook props aren't posted this far in advance — only season-long futures exist right now — so this calibration check will get sharper and more frequent as real weekly lines appear closer to each game.
