How this projection is built: each player's baseline is their actual 2025 regular-season per-game line (Pro Football Reference). Picking a 2026 Week up top pulls that team's real opponent and home/away status from the official 2026 schedule, and auto-sets the defense-strength slider from that opponent's actual 2025 defensive performance (pass yards allowed for QB/WR/TE, rush yards allowed for RB) — you can still nudge it manually. The Coverage Shell slider is now driven by real per-play charting data — each defense's actual 2025 man vs. zone rate and dominant coverage shell (Cover 1/2/3/etc.), sourced from FTN Data via nflverse — compared against the league average to decide man-heavy / balanced / zone-heavy, not a guess. QB projections also get a small real-data adjustment for the opponent's actual pressure rate (more pressure → fewer clean throws, more INT risk). Game script, pace, weather and role share stay as manual situational levers layered on top. For WRs, the opponent's current top corner shows up once a week is picked, with an optional "Elite CB Shadow" toggle — real defenses don't announce shadow assignments in advance, so treat that as an illustrative what-if, unlike the coverage-shell data above it. For committee backfields, "Backfield Split" shifts share between the two backs. Player-level coverage splits: 143 of 189 players (a green checkmark line appears on their card) have enough of their own 2023-25 sample (30+ targets or 60+ pass attempts against both man and zone) that the Coverage Shell slider uses their actual real man-vs-zone performance instead of a generic man-beater/zone-beater tag — normalized to their own 3-year average and capped to stop small-sample outliers from swinging things too far. Players without that badge still use the general tag. This models directional situational impact, not a play-by-play simulation.
Coverage data credit: team-level man/zone rates, player-level performance splits, and pressure rates are charted play-by-play by FTN Data, distributed via nflverse under CC-BY-SA 4.0. Team rates: 2025 season. Player splits: 2023-2025 combined, min. 30 targets / 60 attempts per coverage type.
