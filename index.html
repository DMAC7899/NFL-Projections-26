Scope: 300 players across QB/RB/WR/TE for all 32 teams, including 2026 draft rookies with a clear Year-1 role — one lead player per role plus real committee backfields/WR depth, not literal full rosters. Rosters: reflect confirmed 2026 trades, free-agency moves and coaching hires as of early August 2026. Schedule: official 2026 NFL regular-season grid. Defense tiers: passD derived from each team's ESPN passing yards allowed per game, 2025 full season plus 2026 Weeks 1-7 (refreshed Oct 28, 2026). rushD is a real, weighted 2024+2025+2026 blend (1.5x weight on 2025 and on 2026, matching the same weighting already used for player baselines; CLE/LAR pass tiers keep the manual Garrett-trade adjustment) after finding the 2025-only tier moved 20 of 32 teams once 2024 was properly incorporated — a real, meaningful shift, not noise (e.g. New Orleans and Carolina's real, historically poor 2024 run defense was invisible in a 2025-only view). Coverage shell: real 2025 man/zone rates from PFF's Defense Coverage Scheme report, snap-weighted across every real defender (replaces an earlier FTN/nflverse-sourced table that was found to systematically overstate man rate by an average of 7.5 points league-wide). Pressure rate: real ESPN sack-rate data (sacks per real dropback, 2025 season blended with 2026 Weeks 1-4, all 32 teams), replacing FTN Data via nflverse's broader pressure-rate definition. Time-to-throw comes from FTN Data via nflverse (2025 full season; FTN approved for this field by the owner, Oct 7, 2026). The same applies to the Cover 0-4 shell rates; FTN's 2026 coverage data is not published until after the season, so both stay on 2025. Rankings/Compare auto-apply the real schedule-driven opponent, defense tier, coverage shell and pressure rate for a selected week (game script and pace stay neutral) — open a player in Browse mode to fine-tune further. Best Matchups ranks every player by how much their real matchup this week beats their own neutral-matchup baseline — a QB/RB/TE getting a boost from a real weak defense, favorable real coverage-shell match, or absence of a shadow-caliber corner, not just who scores the most points overall. New coordinators can shift a defense's scheme from what's shown here; the coaching notes on each player flag where that risk is highest. Season Total is checked directly against 17 real 2026 sportsbook season props (DraftKings/BetMGM/consensus). Where a real prop exists for a player (17 players currently), it's shown directly under their Season Total so you can see the actual gap, not just take the number on faith. Weekly (single-game) sportsbook props aren't posted this far in advance — only season-long futures exist right now — so this calibration check will get sharper and more frequent as real weekly lines appear closer to each game.
