Baseline. Each player's core stats (passing/rushing/receiving volume) reflect real per-game production. Selecting a Week pulls the true 2026 opponent and site from the official schedule and sets the defense-strength slider from that opponent's 2025 performance — adjust manually as needed.

Coverage Shell reflects each defense's actual man/zone rate and preferred shell, weighed against the league average. QB efficiency is further adjusted for the opponent's pressure rate. Game script, pace, weather and role share remain manual levers.

Elite CB Shadow surfaces the opponent's top corner and scales the penalty to their 2025 PFF grade rather than a flat estimate. It activates automatically when a top-third corner faces a man-heavy defense, though field-side alignment isn't publicly tracked and so isn't factored in.

Coverage splits for 122 of 189 skill-position players draw on two independent sources — nflverse/FTN charting (2023–25) and PFF's Receiving vs. Scheme report (2025) — averaged where both qualify, since the two sources agree on direction only about three times in four. Values are capped to limit small-sample distortion; players without a qualifying sample use a general man-beater/zone-beater tag instead.

Season Total is a 17-game full-season projection, carrying each team's win total, PFF pass/run-block grade, and sack rate as adjustments to output — not usage. Weekly sportsbook lines aren't posted this far ahead, so this remains a season-level calibration.

Sack conversion is a real 2025 team-defense metric, aggregated from every individual pass-rusher's sacks and total pressures (PFF Pass Rush Summary) up to a team total — sacks divided by pressures generated. Two defenses can pressure the QB at the same rate but finish very differently (real 2025 range: 9.4% to 20.5%), so this is layered on top of the existing opponent-pressure effect on QB passing output, not a replacement for it.

PFF context shown on player cards — passing grade, Big-Time-Throw and Turnover-Worthy-Play rate, route data, ADOT, EPA — largely restates skill already reflected in the underlying stat line, so it informs rather than adjusts the projection.

2025 Schedule Faced, shown per position, is a genuine full-season average recomputed from PFF's real weekly Strength of Schedule data — describing how tough the schedule was that produced each player's actual 2025 numbers. It's backward-looking only, not a 2026 forecast, so it's shown for context rather than folded into the projection.

Play-Action Rate (QB cards) is a real 2025 measure of how often each QB used play-action, and how their yards/attempt and grade compared with and without it — informational context, not a projection input.
Coverage and pressure data charted by FTN Data, distributed via nflverse under CC-BY-SA 4.0 — team rates for 2025, player splits 2023–2025 (minimum 30 targets or 60 attempts per coverage type). QB grades, pressure metrics, route and efficiency data, red-zone shares, and O-line grades via PFF Premium Stats (2025, minimum 6 games played). Win-total and red-zone data current as of early August 2026.

Scope: 189 players across QB/RB/WR/TE for all 32 teams, including 2026 draft rookies with a clear Year-1 role — one lead player per role plus real committee backfields/WR depth, not literal full rosters. Rosters: reflect confirmed 2026 trades, free-agency moves and coaching hires as of early August 2026. Schedule: official 2026 NFL regular-season grid. Defense tiers: derived from each team's actual 2025 passing/rushing yards allowed — a proxy, since 2026 defensive performance doesn't exist yet. Coverage shell & pressure rate: real 2025 per-play charting data from FTN Data via nflverse (CC-BY-SA 4.0) — actual man/zone rates and pressure rates per defense, not estimates. Rankings/Compare auto-apply the real schedule-driven opponent, defense tier, coverage shell and pressure rate for a selected week (game script and pace stay neutral) — open a player in Browse mode to fine-tune further. Best Matchups ranks every player by how much their real matchup this week beats their own neutral-matchup baseline — a QB/RB/TE getting a boost from a real weak defense, favorable real coverage-shell match, or absence of a shadow-caliber corner, not just who scores the most points overall. New coordinators can shift a defense's scheme from what's shown here; the coaching notes on each player flag where that risk is highest. Season Total is checked directly against 17 real 2026 sportsbook season props (DraftKings/BetMGM/consensus). Where a real prop exists for a player (17 players currently), it's shown directly under their Season Total so you can see the actual gap, not just take the number on faith. Weekly (single-game) sportsbook props aren't posted this far in advance — only season-long futures exist right now — so this calibration check will get sharper and more frequent as real weekly lines appear closer to each game.
