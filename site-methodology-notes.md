# Site methodology text (removed from the UI Oct 14, 2026; kept here as back-end reference)

Data &amp; methodology ▸ Tap to expand Baseline. Each player's core stats (passing/rushing/receiving volume) reflect real per-game production. Selecting a Week pulls the true 2026 opponent and site from the official schedule and sets the defense-strength slider from that opponent's 2025 performance — adjust manually as needed. Coverage Shell reflects each defense's actual man/zone rate and preferred shell, weighed against the league average. QB efficiency is further adjusted for the opponent's pressure rate. Game script, pace, weather and role share remain manual levers. Elite CB Shadow surfaces the opponent's top corner and scales the penalty to their 2025 PFF grade rather than a flat estimate. It activates automatically when a top-third corner faces a man-heavy defense, though field-side alignment isn't publicly tracked and so isn't factored in. Coverage splits for 122 of 189 skill-position players draw on two independent sources — nflverse/FTN charting (2023–25) and PFF's Receiving vs. Scheme report (2025) — averaged where both qualify, since the two sources agree on direction only about three times in four. Values are capped to limit small-sample distortion; players without a qualifying sample use a general man-beater/zone-beater tag instead. Season Total is a 17-game full-season projection, carrying each team's win total, PFF pass/run-block grade, and sack rate as adjustments to output — not usage. Weekly sportsbook lines aren't posted this far ahead, so this remains a season-level calibration. Sack conversion is a real team-defense metric (2025 full season plus 2026 so far), aggregated from every individual pass-rusher's sacks and total pressures (PFF Pass Rush Summary) up to a team total — sacks divided by pressures generated. Two defenses can pressure the QB at the same rate but finish very differently (real 2025 range: 9.4% to 20.5%), so this is layered on top of the existing opponent-pressure effect on QB passing output, not a replacement for it. PFF context shown on player cards — passing grade, Big-Time-Throw and Turnover-Worthy-Play rate, route data, ADOT, EPA — largely restates skill already reflected in the underlying stat line, so it informs rather than adjusts the projection. 2025 Schedule Faced, shown per position, is a genuine full-season average recomputed from PFF's real weekly Strength of Schedule data — describing how tough the schedule was that produced each player's actual 2025 numbers. It's backward-looking only, not a 2026 forecast, so it's shown for context rather than folded into the projection. Play-Action Rate (QB cards) is a real 2025 measure of how often each QB used play-action, and how their yards/attempt and grade compared with and without it — informational context, not a projection input. Team man/zone rates from PFF's Defense Coverage Scheme report (2025 season, snap-weighted across every real defender who logged a coverage snap). Pressure rate is ESPN sack rate (2025 with 2026 so far); time-to-throw and Cover 0-4 shell rates come from FTN Data via nflverse (2025 full season). Player splits 2023-2025 (minimum 30 targets or 60 attempts per coverage type). QB grades, pressure metrics, route and efficiency data, red-zone shares, and O-line grades via PFF Premium Stats (2025, minimum 6 games played). Win-total and red-zone data current as of early August 2026; 11 team win totals (and the scoring multiplier built from them) were corrected to the preseason sportsbook consensus on Oct 7, 2026.

Data &amp; methodology ▸ Tap to expand Pick a week and a specific game, then enter the current real Vegas total and spread (this is a static page with no server, so it can't pull live sportsbook lines automatically). The page compares that real line against this tool's own projected total and margin, and colors the comparison green or red depending on which way it points. Our projection here comes from each team's real, current Vegas win total, run through a genuine relationship built from real, complete 2025 season data (every team's real final win total vs. their real average points scored and allowed) &mdash; about half the real variance in scoring is explained by win total alone (R&sup2; = 0.50 offense, 0.60 defense), which is a real, validated signal. It replaced an earlier version that summed individual player projections into a team total, which tested at essentially zero correlation with real results and produced visibly wrong outputs (a bad team shown favored over a good one). This version is meaningfully better, but a win total can itself be wrong, and this doesn't know about a specific week's injuries, weather, or a real line move &mdash; treat it as a real, useful reference point, not a guarantee. Update (Oct 7, 2026): both sides now blend in real, current PFF 2026 team grades through Week 4 (read directly from PFF's own team grades page via Chrome, with the user logged in -- not a screenshot, so no transcription risk) at a real, disclosed 25% weight alongside the season-long win-total estimate: defense checked against real points allowed per game (R&sup2; = 0.08 after four games), offense against real points scored (R&sup2; = 0.66, now stronger than the win-total offense estimate) -- so the weight is still held at 25% for both sides this week and the offense side is the first candidate to be raised if it holds. The defence slope is flatter, so it moves the projection less. Engine layer (Oct 7, 2026): team points now also run through the same player projections used on every other page. (1) Opponent matchup: the team's QB1, RB1-2, WR1-3 and TE1 are projected against the real opponent (defence tiers, run defence, coverage, pressure, sack rate, time-to-throw, OL ratings, corner shadow) and against a neutral defence; half the resulting swing is applied, capped at &plusmn;12%. (2) Injuries: the lineup is rebuilt from the best available players and 60% of the lost projected PPR comes off team points (teammates absorb the rest), floored at -20%; a QB change with real PFF grades still uses the grade ratio instead. Check on Weeks 1-4 results: team-score error 7.63 &rarr; 7.53 pts, total error 11.89 &rarr; 11.67, margin error 9.59 &rarr; 9.50 (in-sample, injuries not replayed). Defensive injuries are now covered: every team carries its key defenders (top 3 ED, 3 DI, 3 LB, 4 CB, 3 S by 2026 snaps) valued by blended 2025+2026 PFF grade; an out defender costs (grade &minus; 55) &times; snap share, converted at 1.6% per team-grade point, split into pass and run effects, and feeds the player projections (pass yards/TD/receiving and rushing) as well as team points. OUT/IR counts 100%, doubtful 75%, questionable 20% (ESPN report, Oct 7). A star edge or corner out is roughly +0.5 to +1 point allowed. The scale is a judgment call (no past weeks to backtest) so revisit as weeks accumulate. Not covered: weather and stale preseason win totals.
